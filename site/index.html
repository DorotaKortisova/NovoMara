Architecture / Landscape

Visitors centre Demänovská dolina

Year
2023
Location
Low Tatras, Slovakia
Outcome
Competition · Second place

Restraint, heritage & landscape

A proposal for a compact visitor centre in a protected natural area, retaining the original 1930s entrance building and reusing structural elements in a modular timber design.

Administrative, visitor and exhibition functions are brought together through passive design and spatial layering, with a sloping roof that responds to the terrain.

Urbanism / Public space

Revitalization of a public square

Period
2018–2024
Location
Malacky, Slovakia
Outcome
First place · Partial realisation

Water as a spatial interface

The revitalization addresses a public square above an underground parking structure. A vertical water wall acts as a fountain, a cooling device and a spatial interface between the new development and the existing urban fabric.

Locally sourced sandstone and planting around the perimeter articulate the square’s material and environmental character.

Research profile

The work investigates how architecture adapts to climate pressure, tourism expansion and changing construction systems in fragile alpine regions, with a focus on mountain huts, ski infrastructure and modernist heritage.

International research includes Fulbright research at the University of Colorado Denver and the Laboratory of Alpine History at Università della Svizzera italiana in Mendrisio.
