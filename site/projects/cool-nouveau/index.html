COOL Nouveau

Year
2022
Location
Bratislava
Role

From the urban ring to ornament

An exhibition interpreting Art Nouveau in Bratislava through urban concepts, architecture and ornament. Curved textiles, 3D-printed models and a circular city model translate its motifs into an exhibition space.
