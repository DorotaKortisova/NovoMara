Vladimír Dedeček

Period
2015–2017
Project
Interpretation of his architecture
Outcome
Publication collaboration

Reading architecture through interpretation

Collaboration on a publication interpreting four significant works by Vladimír Dedeček through text, architecture and photography.

The publication brings together different approaches to reading architectural work and opens a discussion of Dedeček’s buildings and their wider cultural context.

Project notes & credits

Edited by Monika Mitášová, with Marian Zervan, Benjamín Brádňanský, Vít Halada and Hertha Hurnaus.

The supplied portfolio records a Slovak publication in 2017 and an English edition in 2018.
