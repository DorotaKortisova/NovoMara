Modernism Revisited 2026

Period
2025–2026
Location
Trenčín, Slovakia
Role
Conference director

Typology · Topography · Strategy

Modernism’s ideas, infrastructures and environments continue to shape how we live. The project considers how these legacies can be reinterpreted and reactivated amid ecological transitions, demographic shifts and changing public infrastructures.
