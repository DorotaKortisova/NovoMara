House ISB

Year
2020
Place
Near Brno, Czech Republic
Project
Rural house renovation

A clear plan, two different frontages

A renovation proposal for a rural terraced house with a large garden. Earlier extensions had created a fragmented sequence of rooms.

The design clarifies the floor plan while retaining the overall floor area. The house keeps its rural appearance toward the street and gains a contemporary garden-facing frontage.
