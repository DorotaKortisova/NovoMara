Housing and the identity of the city

An article examining residential construction in Bratislava during the twentieth and twenty-first centuries, with a focus on post-war concrete-panel housing.

The research considers mass renovation, the intensification of existing residential areas, changing apartment layouts and the relationship between housing and urban infrastructure.
